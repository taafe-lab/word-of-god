# The Word of God

A private Bible study app for iPhone that works offline.

- **Come and See**: 60 days with Jesus in John's Gospel (Come, See, Follow).
- **The Big Story**: the Bible's whole storyline (Session 1 ready).
- **Mark**: a complete book study in nine sessions, with a guide and a quiz.

Everything written in the app is saved on the phone only. Nothing is sent to this repository or anywhere else.

To install: open https://taafe-lab.github.io/word-of-god/ in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once from the Home Screen icon while online so it can save itself for offline use.
